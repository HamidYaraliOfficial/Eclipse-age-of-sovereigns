# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — Initial playable foundation

- Added a procedural stylized 3D hex-map with city markers and selectable land tiles.
- Added four nations, nine city locations, economy, turn advancement, construction and population updates.
- Added starter technologies, research progression, diplomacy actions, infantry recruitment and tactical battle decisions.
- Added responsive UI in English, Persian and Simplified Chinese.
- Added browser-local save/load and JSON save import/export validation.
- Added a custom all-rights-reserved license and multilingual documentation.
- Added automated engine tests.

## Known scope limits

- This version is a playable foundation, not the complete AAA-scale design originally envisioned.
- The world has four nations and nine authored city locations; the 40+ nation / 150+ city world is future work.
- The research catalogue is a starter set, not a fully authored 200-technology tree.
- Tactical combat resolves through command choices and battle-state simulation, with a 3D tactical scene rather than individual pathfinding soldiers.
- Multiplayer, full campaign missions, full espionage/politics/logistics simulations and advanced audio are not yet implemented.
